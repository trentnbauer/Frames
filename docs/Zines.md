# Zines

A zine is a printable booklet. Zines are listed on the **Compositions** page: choose **Zines** in the bar under the title (on a phone, they are listed under your compositions). Start one there with **New zine**. To start one from photographs, select them in the Library or a shoot, choose **Add to composition…**, then **Zine**. Start a new zine, or pick an existing one to add the photos to.

A zine opens in the same editor as a composition: **← Compositions** and the name (click it to rename) on the left, the bin to remove it, and **Progress · Review · Done** on the right. The pages sit on the wall, the panel beside it shows the chosen page (or the zine's settings when no page is chosen), and the candidates are below.

The photographs you add become **candidates**, as in a composition. They're shown below the pages. Drag a candidate onto a page to place it, or drag a photo from one page onto another to swap them (onto an empty slot, it moves). Each candidate shows the pages it's on ("Page 4", "Pages 2–3") or **Not placed**. **Fill in order** puts the candidates that are not placed yet into the empty slots, in order; it can be undone from the message that appears. **Add photos…** adds more candidates from the library. When you choose a photo for a page, candidates already on a page are marked with where they are. **Remove** (shown when you point at a candidate) takes a candidate out of the zine and off its pages; the photograph stays in Frames.

A zine is printed on **one sheet**, A3 or A4, and folds into **8 pages**. Removing a page adds an empty one at the end; **Undo** in the message puts it back. Changing a page to *Across two pages* can push pages off the end; their photos stay candidates.

## Pages and spreads

The wall shows the zine in book order, the way a reader sees it: page 1, the cover, on its own on the right, then each pair of facing pages, two spreads a row, then the back cover on its own on the left.

Choose a page, then choose its layout in the panel:

- **One photo**: the photograph fitted inside the margin, not cropped.
- **Two photos**: two photographs, side by side or one above the other, whichever shows more of them, each fitted, not cropped.
- **Three photos**: two portraits side by side and one landscape across the page. Choose whether the portraits or the landscape go on top.
- **Across two pages**: one photograph across both facing pages, cropped to fill them. The margin stays on the outer edges, so home printers that can't print to the edge won't clip it; only the fold is borderless. It always starts on a left-hand page. If it would start on a right-hand page, Frames adds a blank page before it, marked "added".
- **Blank**.

Click a photograph on the chosen page, or press **Choose** or **Change** in the panel, to pick a photograph. The candidates come first, then your library. **← Earlier** and **Later →** reorder pages. Dragging is not available on touch screens, so use these buttons there. Changes save as you make them.

**Text.** Each page can carry a title or caption: turn **Text** on in the panel, type it (up to 280 characters), and choose **Top**, **Middle** or **Bottom** and **Serif** or **Mono**. Over a photograph the text sits on a band of the paper colour so it stays readable. Turning text off hides it but keeps what you typed. On a spread, the text goes on the left page. In the PDF the text uses Instrument Serif and IBM Plex Mono if they are installed on the server, otherwise Georgia and a plain monospace font.

## Size, margin and the PDF

- **Print on**: an A3 sheet (8 A6 pages, 105 × 148 mm) or an A4 sheet (8 A7 pages, 74 × 105 mm). Zines made before this change keep their page size (A5, A6 or Square), with 8, 16 or 24 pages, and print one page per PDF page.
- **Border colour**: white, black or paper. It is the paper colour of every page, in the editor and the PDF. On black, page text is white.
- **Border**: 0–20 per cent of the page width. It is the space round the photographs and between them. Spreads keep it on their outer edges.
- **How to fold**: a small diagram of the sheet, with the three steps.
- **Download PDF** is in the **Done** panel: choose **Done** above the panel, then **Download PDF**. Done is read-only (it warns if any slot is still empty and will print blank); choose **Progress** or **Review** to change the zine again. The PDF is one sheet at 300 dpi, with the 8 pages placed for folding. The top row is upside down: pages 5, 4, 3 and 2 from the left. The bottom row is pages 6, 7, 8 (the back) and 1 (the front). Print it on one side at actual size, not *fit to page*. Crease it into eighths, cut along the short grey line in the middle, then fold it into a booklet with page 1 on the front.

The PDF is made from each photograph's preview (2400 px on its longest side by default, see *Preview quality*). That is about 200 dpi across an A5 page, and a spread is enlarged a little more. Raise the preview size for sharper prints. Your originals are never changed.

The bin by the name removes the zine's layout. Your photographs stay in Frames. Resetting the library removes zines too.
