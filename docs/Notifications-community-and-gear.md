# Notifications, community review and gear

## Notifications

The bell links to guest activity, reviews, composition ideas, discovered gear and other events. **Clear** removes read and unread entries from your own panel, including older entries beyond the first page. **Mark all read** keeps them but clears their unread state. Use an individual **×** to dismiss one event. A failed clear keeps the entries and displays an error; new events can still arrive later.

New camera/lens discoveries have separate **Add to gear** and **Blacklist** actions. Dismissing a notice does not forget the discovery. Nothing is added to owned gear automatically. Each discovery retains its photo count, and known owned gear is excluded. Choose bell/email categories, review reminders and quiet hours under **Settings → Your library → Notifications**.

## Community and other tools

Community review is opt-in, anonymous between members, and separate from your personal and guest scores. Sensitive tags exclude photos from the pool; reports go to administrators. **To do** walks through missing/awaiting photo details. **Stats** summarises your library, **Compare** helps choose between photos, and **Near-duplicates** links variants without deleting originals.
