## Zines

A zine is a printable booklet. Zines are listed on the **Compositions** page, under your compositions. Start one there with **New zine** and a page count (8, 16 or 24). To start one from photographs, select them in the Library or a shoot, choose **Add to composition…**, then **Zine**. Pick a page count for a new zine, or pick an existing zine to add the photos to.

The photographs you add become **candidates**, as in a composition. They're shown below the pages. Drag a candidate onto a page to place it, or drag a photo from one page onto another to swap them (onto an empty slot, it moves). Each candidate shows the pages it's on. **Add photos…** adds more candidates from the library. **Remove** takes a candidate out of the zine and off its pages; the photograph stays in Frames.

A zine keeps the number of pages you chose. Removing a page adds an empty one at the end. Changing a page to *Across two pages* can push pages off the end; their photos stay candidates. Change the count under the page settings.

### Pages and spreads

The editor shows the zine the way a reader sees it: page 1, the cover, on its own on the right, then each pair of facing pages, then the back cover on its own.

Choose a page, then choose its layout in the panel:

- **One photo**: the photograph fitted inside the margin, not cropped.
- **Two photos**: two photographs, stacked on a tall page or side by side on a square one, each fitted, not cropped.
- **Across two pages**: one photograph across both facing pages, edge to edge, cropped to fill them. It always starts on a left-hand page. If it would start on a right-hand page, Frames adds a blank page before it, marked "added".
- **Blank**.

Click a photograph on the chosen page, or press **Choose** or **Change** in the panel, to pick a photograph. The candidates come first, then your library. **Move earlier** and **Move later** reorder pages. Dragging is not available on touch screens, so use these buttons there. Changes save as you make them.

### Size, margin and the PDF

- **Page size**: A5 (148 × 210 mm), A6 (105 × 148 mm) or Square (200 × 200 mm).
- **Margin**: 0–20 per cent of the page width. It is the space round single and double pages and between two photographs on one page. Spreads have no margin.
- **Download PDF**: one page per PDF page, in reading order, at 300 dpi on white. Unused pages at the end print blank. To print a booklet, use your PDF viewer's booklet printing (for example Adobe Acrobat's *Booklet* option), or send the PDF to a print shop.

The PDF is made from each photograph's preview (2400 px on its longest side by default, see *Preview quality*). That is about 200 dpi across an A5 page, and a spread is enlarged a little more. Raise the preview size for sharper prints. Your originals are never changed.

**Remove zine…** removes the zine's layout. Your photographs stay in Frames. Resetting the library removes zines too.
