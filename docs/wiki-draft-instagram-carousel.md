# Wiki draft: Instagram carousel

A new section for the *Compositions* guide (or a page of its own linked from it):

## Instagram carousels

Carousels are listed on the **Compositions** page, under your compositions and zines. Start one there with **New carousel**. To start one from photographs, select them in the Library or a shoot, choose **Add to composition…**, then **Instagram carousel**, and either **Create** a new carousel or pick one you already have. A new carousel starts with one slide per photo, in the order you picked them. Photos added to an existing carousel become candidates.

A carousel opens in the same editor as a composition and a zine: the name and bin on the left, **Progress · Review · Done** on the right, the slides on the wall, the panel beside it and the candidates below. Photos past the 20th slide stay candidates, and the candidates say how many didn't fit.

- **Slides.** The wall shows three slides at a time (one on a phone); the ← → arrows step one slide. Black slides have an outline so they show on the dark wall. Click a slide to choose it, then set its layout:
  - **One photo**: fitted inside the border.
  - **Two photos**: one above the other or side by side, whichever shows more of them (two landscapes stack, two portraits sit side by side), with the border between them as well.
  - **Three photos**: two portraits side by side and one landscape, portraits or landscape on top.
  - **Across two slides**: one photo across two slides, cropped to fit, so it runs on as you swipe. Use it for panoramas. The border stays on the outside edges; only the join is open. When the carousel already has 20 slides this choice is unavailable until you remove one.
  - **Blank**.
- **Moving photos.** **← Earlier** and **Later →** reorder slides, **+ Slide** adds one, up to Instagram's 20, and **Remove this slide** takes one out (with **Undo**). **Fill in order** puts candidates that are not placed into empty slots and then new slides.
- **Candidates.** As in a composition, candidates sit below the slides. Drag one onto a slide to place it, or drag a slide's photo onto another to swap them. On a phone, tap a slot and the candidates are listed first.
- **Shape**: 4:5 (1080×1350, Instagram's tallest feed post and the default), 3:4 (1080×1440) or 1:1 (1080×1080).
- **Border colour**: white, black or paper, the colour round the photos. It and the border width are also in the panel of a chosen slide, marked *All slides*.
- **Border**: 0–20 per cent of the slide's width.
- **Download slides** is in the **Done** panel (choose **Done** above it; Progress or Review reopens the carousel for changes): a zip of JPEGs named `slide-01.jpg`, `slide-02.jpg` and so on, in order.

The slides are made from each photograph's preview (2400 px on its longest side by default, see *Preview quality*), which is already the right way up and developed from RAW. Your originals are never changed. The bin by the name removes the carousel; your photographs stay in Frames.

Posting to Instagram from Frames is not available yet (issue #489).
