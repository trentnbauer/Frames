## Instagram carousels

Carousels are listed on the **Compositions** page, under your compositions and zines. Start one there with **New carousel**. To start one from photographs, select them in the Library or a shoot, choose **Add to composition…**, then **Instagram carousel**, and either **Create** a new carousel or pick one you already have. A new carousel starts with one slide per photo, in the order you picked them. Photos added to an existing carousel become candidates.

A carousel opens in its own editor, which works like a zine's:

- **Slides.** They're shown in a row. Click a slide to choose it, then set its layout:
  - **One photo**: fitted inside the border.
  - **Two photos**: one above the other or side by side, whichever shows more of them (two landscapes stack, two portraits sit side by side), with the border between them as well.
  - **Across two slides**: one photo filling two slides edge to edge, cropped to fit, so it runs on as you swipe. Use it for panoramas.
  - **Blank**.
- **Moving photos.** **Move earlier** and **Move later** reorder slides, and **Add slide** adds one, up to Instagram's 20.
- **Candidates.** As in a composition, candidates sit below the slides. Drag one onto a slide to place it, or drag a slide's photo onto another to swap them. On a phone, tap a slot and the candidates are listed first.
- **Shape**: 4:5 (1080×1350, Instagram's tallest feed post and the default), 3:4 (1080×1440) or 1:1 (1080×1080).
- **Colour**: white, black or paper, the colour round the photos.
- **Border**: 0–20 per cent of the slide's width.
- **Download slides**: a zip of JPEGs named `slide-01.jpg`, `slide-02.jpg` and so on, in order.

The slides are made from each photograph's preview (2400 px on its longest side by default, see *Preview quality*), which is already the right way up and developed from RAW. Your originals are never changed. **Remove carousel…** removes the carousel; your photographs stay in Frames.

Posting to Instagram from Frames is not available yet (issue #489).
