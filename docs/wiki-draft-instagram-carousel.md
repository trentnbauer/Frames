# Wiki draft: Instagram carousel

A new section for the *Library* guide (or a page of its own linked from it):

## Instagram carousel

Select photographs in the Library or in a shoot, choose **Add to composition…**, then **Instagram carousel**. The slides come in the order you picked the photographs. A carousel holds up to 20 slides.

- **Shape.** 4:5 (1080×1350, Instagram's tallest feed post and the default), 3:4 (1080×1440) or 1:1 (1080×1080). Every slide in a carousel has the same shape.
- **Border.** From 0 to 25 per cent of the slide's width, all the way round. Each photograph is fitted inside the border without cropping, and the space left over is the border colour.
- **Colour.** White, black or paper (a warm off-white).
- **Per slide.** *One photo*; *Two photos*, paired in picking order: two landscapes go one above the other and two portraits side by side, whichever shows more of them, with the border between them as well (up to 40 photos); or *Split wide photos*: A wide photograph is laid across as many slides as its width fills, so a panorama scrolls as one picture. The border runs round the whole panorama, not round each slide. A 3:2 landscape takes two slides when this is on.

The previews in the dialog are the real slides. **Download slides** saves a zip of JPEGs named `slide-01.jpg`, `slide-02.jpg` and so on. Where the browser can share files (most phones), **Share…** sends the slides straight to the share sheet, so you can save them to Photos or open them in Instagram.

The slides are made from each photograph's preview (2400 px on its longest side by default, see *Preview quality*), which is already the right way up and developed from RAW. A panorama split across four or more slides is wider than its preview, so it is enlarged a little; raise the preview size if that matters. Nothing is stored: the slides are made each time, and your originals are never changed.

Posting to Instagram from Frames is not available yet.
