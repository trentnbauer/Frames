# Compositions

A composition is a wall of prints planned to scale: print sizes, frames, mats, crops, stacked photographs and the candidates that might fill them. Open **Compositions** to see **Progress → Review → Done**, shown with **circle → diamond → square**.

The bar under the title chooses what is listed: **Frames**, **Zines** or **Carousels** (each with its count), a search, the stage (**Progress**, **Review**, **Done**, with how many of the chosen kind are in each), **Created** or **Updated** order, and the arrow for newest or oldest first. Created follows the order things were made in. The choices stay in the page address, so coming back from a composition shows the same list. The title, New composition and this bar stay visible below the app header while scrolling. On the Zines or Carousels view, the main button (and N) reads **New zine** or **New carousel** and starts one of those. On a phone the same choices are a search, **Frames / Zines / Carousels** tabs, the stage, and an order menu (**Updated** or **Created**, newest or oldest first) beside the count; the + button starts whatever kind is showing.

Composition cards show the saved layout, orientation, frames, mats, crops and both photographs in stacked prints, fitted closely to the preview. Candidate grids use thumbnails; the wall uses larger previews.

## The editor

- **Name:** type directly into the title. Enter or leaving the field saves; Escape cancels; a blank name restores the previous one. The Share dialog uses the current name too.
- **Prints:** add prints, choose their size/orientation and click to select one, then drag it into position. Select a print to change its frame, mat or crop. These previews never alter the original photograph.
- **Candidates:** add photographs from a shoot, Library, search/selection or To do. Select a print, then choose a candidate to fill it. Remove a candidate from this composition without deleting it from the library; if it is currently in a print, Frames asks before clearing that placement.
- **Room photo:** add one from the wall controls, mark a known width and preview the prints at scale. The room picture is kept privately and is not included in share links or the portfolio.
- **Sharpness:** each print shows its print resolution and warns when it falls below the target pixels per inch. Moving a composition to Review or Done asks first if any print is below the sharpness setting; on a phone the same prints are listed on the composition page.
- **Starting sizes:** a new composition starts with print sizes the best photograph in its pool can fill at 300 PPI (A-series sizes only); raise them in the editor. Wall sizes show one decimal everywhere (0.9 × 0.6 m).

The editor title/action bar scrolls with the page; it is deliberately not sticky. This is separate from the sticky Compositions list header.

## Progress, Review and Done

Filling every artwork slot does not change the stage automatically. Choose **Review** when ready to check the wall, then **Done** when finished. Review and Done require every slot, including both halves of a stacked print, to hold a photograph. Emptying a slot while reviewing returns it to Progress.

Done is read-only: the name, layout, candidates, crops, frames and room photo cannot be edited or deleted. The workflow selector stays visible. Choose Progress or Review to reopen it; if artwork is missing, choose Progress first. Zoom, fullscreen and sharing remain available.

**Export** is in the **Done · Read-only** block where the frame-editing controls normally appear, keeping the header actions in place. Export downloads untouched originals individually, in print order; it does not create a ZIP. The Done list also offers the server-generated PDF print sheet listing sizes, frames and photographs. Older compositions marked Printed migrate to Done.

![Done composition with its read-only export panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/trentnbauer/Frames/screenshots/composition-done-dark.png)

Previously printed compositions migrate to Done; other compositions start in Progress. Legacy printed/reopen requests map to Done/Progress. Changing a photo elsewhere in Library can still change what a composition displays. Reopen a Done portfolio backing list before changing its candidates.

## Pan and zoom

Scroll over the composition wall, pinch a trackpad, or use two fingers on a touchscreen to zoom from 50% to 400%. The bottom-left **−** and **+** buttons zoom around the centre. Frames and the background move together immediately during pan and zoom, without animating their positions or sizes behind the pointer. Drag empty wall space to pan. Hold **Space** and drag anywhere on the wall, including over a frame, or drag with the middle mouse button, to pan without moving frames. Click a frame to select it before dragging it to move; pointer movements smaller than six screen pixels do not move it. These controls also work in fullscreen and guest editors. Zoom and pan affect only the current view: print sizes, positions, photo crops and exports stay the same. Scrolling a settings panel keeps scrolling that panel. The view resets when the editor is reopened.

The controls show only −/+; the help text runs beside them along the bottom.

## Ideas and guests

[Composition ideas](Composition-ideas) can start a set from tags, subjects, colours, locations or a similar look. Starting from an existing photograph, shoot or selection uses exactly those starting candidates.

A shared composition can allow ratings, downloads and layout/photo/frame suggestions. Guest edits are a proposed copy: they change nothing until you approve them. See [Share links](Share-links).
