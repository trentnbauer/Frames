# Composition ideas

Home's **Ideas for you** and the suggested set on Compositions use the same engine. An idea needs at least **three eligible photographs** sharing a usable feature. It can come from **one photoshoot** and can include **unrated photographs**; you do not need AI embeddings or a completed review schedule first.

![Home with a review banner and composition ideas](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/trentnbauer/Frames/screenshots/home-dark.png)

Ideas can use:

- Accepted **subjects and tags**.
- **Colours** read from the photographs during image analysis.
- A shared **named location**.
- Nearby GPS coordinates without a place name, grouped into small geographic cells of roughly 2 km.
- **Visual similarity**, using local image features and optional AI embeddings.

Each idea explains why its photos belong together. Different reasons are rotated through so a heavily tagged library can still show colour and location ideas. Similar photo sets are deduplicated. Ideas using accepted AI tags or embeddings show **✦**; ordinary colour/location rules do not acquire an AI marker.

**Start this composition** creates a composition with the suggested candidates. **Not for me** records a rejection so those candidates do not come back as composition ideas. Missing photographs, variants, unaccepted/rejected tags and photos with an average rating below 3.5 do not qualify. The engine considers up to 400 eligible photographs, prioritised by learned preferences, and returns up to six ideas.

If no idea appears, check for at least three eligible photos sharing an accepted tag/subject, analysed colour or usable location. A library with no shared feature can legitimately show **No ideas yet**.

Ideas can come from one photoshoot and include unrated photos; completed reviews and AI embeddings are not required.
