# Notebooks

A notebook lays your photographs out like an open pocket notebook: each photograph on a right-hand page, and facing it a left-hand page with its number (01, 02, …) and a short note, or nothing.

Notebooks are listed on the **Compositions** page: choose **Notebooks** in the bar under the title (on a phone, the **Notebooks** tab), and start one with **New notebook**. To start one from photographs, select them in the Library or a shoot, choose **Add to composition…**, then **Notebook**, and either **Create** a new notebook or pick one you have. A new notebook starts with one page per photo, in the order you picked them. Photos added to an existing notebook become candidates.

It opens in the same editor as a zine: the name and bin on the left, **Progress · Review · Done** on the right, the pages on the wall, one pair a row, the panel beside it and the candidates below.

- **Paper**: Pocket (90 × 140 mm), A6 (the default) or A5. It sets the shape of the pages on the wall.
- **Photo page.** The photo is fitted inside the border. A landscape photo is turned a quarter-turn clockwise so it fills the portrait page; you turn the notebook to look at it.
- **Facing page.** Click either page of a pair to choose it. **Number and note** shows the number big at the top and your note (up to 280 characters, serif or mono) small at the bottom. **Blank** leaves the page empty.
- **Moving photos.** **← Earlier** and **Later →** reorder pages and the numbers follow. **+ Page** adds one, up to 100, and **Remove this page** takes one out (with **Undo**). **Fill in order** puts candidates that are not placed into empty pages and then adds new ones. Drag a candidate onto a page, or one page's photo onto another to swap them.
- **Border colour** and **Border** width apply to every page.
- **Download originals** is in the **Done** panel. It saves the original file of each photo, numbered in page order (`1_name.jpg`, `2_name.jpg`, …). The files are not changed or turned: the turning and the notes are only how the notebook is shown. There is no PDF.

The bin by the name removes the notebook. Your photographs stay in Frames.
