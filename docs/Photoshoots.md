# Photoshoots

A photoshoot groups photographs from an import, day or roll. Use its page to review, share or start a composition from that shoot.

## Dates and search

Click a photoshoot’s date to apply a new date to every photograph in that shoot. This updates the displayed date without changing the camera date or original file; another shoot is unaffected. Keyword search accepts phrases such as **milky way at Cape Schanck**, combining tags and locations without searching for the connecting word. This does not add typo correction.

## Empty shoots

Photoshoots are automatically removed when their last photo is deleted from Frames or moved to another shoot, together with their shoot share links. Existing empty shoots are cleaned up on upgrade. Shoots containing archived or missing-file records are retained; only a genuinely empty shoot is removed. A newly created empty shoot can still be filled, and a scan that imports no photos does not leave an empty shoot behind. This cleanup removes database records only; it does not remove original files.

## Sharing

Photoshoot pages have a Share button for independent links, permissions, expiry, PINs and guest reviews. See [Share links](Share-links).

## Shoot actions

Open the **⋯** menu in a shoot’s header for **Analyze with AI** or **Delete entire shoot**. Deletion uses an in-app confirmation that explains the photo count and effects: the shoot and all its photos leave Frames, reviews/tags/composition placements are removed, and shoot share links stop working. Original files stay untouched; recovery follows the configured retention period. Cancel or Escape leaves the shoot unchanged.

Press **A** on Home to go to Shoots with **Add photos** already open. The upload request waits for storage settings to load, so it also works on a cold page load. **A** on Shoots opens the same modal in place.
